During my internship at Cardtree, as one of the founding designers, I contributed across the core product and the newly launched AI Twin. I designed and shipped multiple new widgets and user-facing experiences, while refining legacy components to create a cohesive system. My work also included working closely with developers to ensure design quality in implementation.

## Context
Cardtree helps users, especially people who are tech-naive create a professional one-page website using their existing LinkedIn profile.
The challenge was to **scale Cardtree from a simple profile builder to a fully customizable profile + AI-powered personal twin, while keeping the UX simple for non-tech users.**
