## Real-Time Safety & In-Transit Tracking UI

Transitioning into an Associate Professional role, I took ownership of the live trip tracking experience. During active rides, both riders and drivers need real-time situational awareness without being distracted by unnecessary alerts.

We redesigned the live trip map interface to prioritize key trip telemetry: pick-up sequence, estimated drop-offs, and emergency contact triggers.

### Live Telemetry & Map Controls

- **Progressive Disclosure**: Detailed passenger drop-off lists remain collapsed until approaching the next destination.
- **One-Tap Emergency Action**: Prominent safety shield widget integrated directly into the live map screen.
- **Audio Cues**: Subtle tactile and sound feedback when riders complete their pick-up confirmation.
