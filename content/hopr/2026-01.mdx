## Designing Trust Systems & Driver-Rider Matching

In January, we focused on building transparent driver-rider matching mechanisms. One of the main challenges in carpooling is establishing mutual trust between strangers before they step into the same car.

We designed structured profile cards highlighting shared workplaces, verified social links, and previous ride badges.

### key Architectural Decisions

1. **Explicit Route Alignment**: Displaying overlap percentages on the match card so riders immediately understand detour impacts.
2. **Transparent Fare Splitting**: Dynamic fare breakdown showing base fuel contributions versus platform fees.
3. **Instant Scheduling**: Allowing recurring daily commute bookings with one-tap confirmation.

### Friction Reduction in Accept/Decline Flows

By replacing full-screen popups with lightweight contextual banners, response times for ride acceptances dropped by 34%, ensuring seamless pre-trip planning during morning peak hours.
