## Behavioral Feedback Loops & System Standardization

In April, we focused on post-ride feedback dynamics and scaling the component library across Rapido’s ecosystem. Traditional star ratings fail to capture the nuanced interpersonal etiquette of shared carpooling.

We introduced behavior-based badges (such as *Punctual Commuter*, *Great Conversation*, or *Quiet Ride Preference*) that reinforce positive social norms within the Hopr community.

### Design System Components

1. **Badge Selector Primitives**: Accessible micro-chips supporting quick multi-selection during rating flows.
2. **Commute Analytics Dashboard**: Weekly savings summary (fuel saved, CO₂ reduction, and money saved).
3. **Responsive Mobile Token System**: Standardized spacing and typography tokens aligned with Rapido's unified design system.
