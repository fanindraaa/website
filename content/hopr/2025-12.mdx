## Joining Hopr & Defining the Core Mental Model

I joined Hopr as an intern to explore how trust, behavior, and interaction design can shape adoption in an emerging shared-mobility category. Carpooling in urban India comes with unique cultural and logistical constraints—passengers value predictability and safety, while drivers seek low friction and equitable costs.

Our initial focus was rethinking the onboarding mental model. Rather than treating carpooling like standard ride-hailing, we set out to design a peer-to-peer verification and intent-matching flow.

> "Designing for shared mobility isn't just about routing vehicles—it's about building mutual accountability before the journey even begins."

### Primary Research Insights

- **Safety & Verification**: 78% of early survey respondents prioritized identity verification over ETA speed.
- **Route Affinity**: Users prefer matching with commuters who share identical work schedules and drop-off corridors.
- **Micro-Interactions**: Visual cues during profile setup significantly increased complete onboarding rates.
